Scores are the share of the fleet beaten: (fleet − place) ÷ (fleet − 1), so first is 100% and last is 0%. A season score is the mean over that year's events. Where J/105s raced in a mixed handicap class they are ranked among the J/105s only and marked "mixed"; those count toward season scores only with at least 3 J/105s.

Hulls are followed by sail number. Typos and PHRF numbers are mapped onto hulls only with a recorded reason; some numbers are still shared by more than one boat and are under review. Names are as printed in each event's results. Every result links to its official page.
