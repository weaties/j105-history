CYC Sound Wednesday Night Series results from Clubspot with Clubspot's own throwouts: 43 races scored in 2026 (races 6, 15, 18 and 33 not sailed) and 39 in 2025. Some 2025 race dates are taken from when the race was entered in Clubspot.

Ballard Cup results from Sloop Tavern YC. In 2026 the J/105s sailed Division 6 in Series I and Division 7 in Series II and III; race scores are rebuilt from corrected times (low point, ties sharing places, DNC as entries + 1, DNS, DNF or RET as boats that came to the start + 1, one throwout) and match the official totals for every boat. In 2025 the J/105s sailed Division 6 and the scores are STYC's published race-by-race scores. Boat names follow each year's results (for example Double Trouble in 2025 became Debonair in 2026); "Mazu (Counterpoint)" and "Creative / Bluelight" are entry names used in one series.

Charts follow the running total before throwouts; the final column and the table rank on net points after throwouts. When series are combined, a boat that was not entered in one of them takes that series' DNC score for each of its races and the usual number of throwouts, shown as – in the table. Race Week has its own page.

Codes: DNC did not come to the start, DNS did not start, DNF did not finish, RET retired, OCS on course side, DSQ disqualified, ZFP 20% penalty, SCP scoring penalty, CB as scored by STYC.
