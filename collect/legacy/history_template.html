Sources: Ballard Cup results from Sloop Tavern YC (2014 to 2026, Series I and II not sailed in 2020; before 2018 the J/105s raced in mixed handicap classes and are ranked here among the J/105s only). CYC Sound Wednesday Night Series, CYC Puget Sound Spring Regatta (2024 Big Boat weekend, 2025, 2026), PSSC Big Boat, the 2026 PSSC Small Boat & J/Fest and the 2026 North Americans from Clubspot (2024 onward). Race Week from Yacht Scoring: Whidbey Island 2012 to 2019 (no J/105 class in 2015), Anacortes 2021 to 2024 and Bellingham 2025 and 2026, plus J/Fest Seattle 2024. CYC Wednesdays and the Spring Regatta before 2024 were scored somewhere we have not found yet.

The 2026 NA visitors filter covers the eight boats that came from out of town for the North Americans (Niuhi, Ne*Ne, Arbitrage, Deja Voodoo, Chao Pescao, Blue Flash, Kinetic #401 and Kinderspel²). Filtering hides boats but keeps the official places, so a score still reflects the whole fleet that raced.

Places are final series places after throwouts. Names and skippers are as entered in each event's results; the skipper of record for a year is the one named most often, so club and shared boats change skipper often. Owners are not published, so ownership changes are inferred from renames and new skippers. A few sail numbers were entered inconsistently and are merged: Creative (51911, 686, 422), LiftOff (403, 404) and Cyrano (253, 255, 258).
